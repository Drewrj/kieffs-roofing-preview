About Kieff's Roofing

A Family Business Built on Roofs That Last

Kieff’s Roofing was started in Stanton in 1960. Sixty-five years later, the company is still family-owned and -operated — Brad, Brian and Jarrod Kieff lead the same crews their father and grandfather built.

We’re a Mid-Michigan company through and through. We’ve roofed houses in three generations of the same family, set trusses on barns we’ll drive past for decades, and replaced more gutters in Montcalm County than we can count. We’d rather earn the next job than chase the last one.
